# presentation-work

Presentation decks by Tarunima Vijay, covering operations strategy and AI governance.

## Decks

### 1. Operations Strategy & Executive Reporting
A consulting-style case study for a fictional company, built with synthetic data to show how I approach operations work. It covers market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM), a diagnostic of operating friction, a value-stream analysis, an executive performance view, a target operating model and a 90-day roadmap.

### 2. AI Incident Repository
Governance lessons from six high-consequence AI incidents, with each case mapped to ISO/IEC 42001, the NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act. Each case follows the same path: incident evidence, system behaviour, risk and controls, and governance response.
